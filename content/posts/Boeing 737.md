The Boeing 737 MAX is an aircraft developed by Boeing commercial Airplanes, it is an upgraded version of Boeing 737. It is a narrow body airliner. On July 20, 2011, they announced plans for third major upgrade. So this is the fourth generation of the Boeing 737. It includes four main variants MAX 7, MAX 8, MAX 9 and MAX 10. Typically Boeing is offering 138 to 230 seats and range of 5,954 to 7,084 km.

On August 30, 2011, Boeing launched his third upgraded of 737 new engine varient,  called Boeing 737 MAX. And the 737 MAX had its first flight on January 29, 2016 and certified on 2017. And the first delivery of MAX 8 was on May 6, 2017 to Malindo Air and received over 5,000 orders by 2019. The primary goal of Boeing 737 MAX was to improve fuel efficiency by using more efficient engines, which is advanced CFM LEAP - 1B engines. That is providing a 20% reduction in CO2 emissions and fuel usages. But due to this engines one issue had come forward that in certain condition the nose of aircraft goes upward. To solve to issue, Boeing made a software system called Maneuvering Characteristics Augmentation System (MCAS).


Maneuvering Characteristics Augmentation System (MCAS) is designed to automatically push the nose of the plane down. It activates when the plane’s system thinks there is a risk of a stall. It's goal is to keep the aircraft stable and safe. One day, MCAS kept pushing the plane’s nose down again and again. The pilots tried to control the aircraft, but the system kept taking over their actions. Another big problem was that pilots were not clearly told about MCAS. Many didn’t even know it was there, and they were not trained on how to deal with it.

The main problem was that MCAS depended on data from only one sensor (angle-of-attack sensor). If this sensor gave wrong data, the system would still turn on. This is what happened in both crashes. The sensor sent wrong signals. The system thought the plane would stall, even when it was not going to stall.

The results of this failure were very serious. The Boeing 737 MAX disaster is really bad. It is one of the most serious things that has happened in aviation in a long time. The Boeing 737 MAX was involved in two plane crashes. The first one was Lion Air Flight 610, which happened in 2018. The second one was Ethiopian Airlines Flight 302, which happened in 2019. These crashes were very sad because they killed 346 people. This led to all Boeing 737 MAX planes being grounded around the world. Boeing had to deal with big financial losses and many legal problems. The company’s reputation was badly affected. People started to lose trust in the safety of air travel and in Boeing 737 MAX planes. The planes were not allowed to fly because of these crashes.

The main reason for the failure was a problem in design and software, not just hardware. Even though a faulty sensor started the issue, the bigger mistake was that the system depended on only one sensor without any backup or checking. The software did not compare data from other sensors, which is very important for safety. There was also poor communication and not enough training given to pilots.

In short, the Boeing 737 MAX disaster shows how important it is to have safe software design, proper testing, and clear communication, especially in critical fields like aviation.

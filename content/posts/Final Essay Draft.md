#### Introduction of Semiconductors
A  semiconductor is a material which acts as an insulator or conductor which is used in making electronic devices, such as blocks of computers and integrated circuits and also used in present digital technology. Semiconductors work as small electrical switches which repeatedly turn off and on the flow of electricity. It was widely used in many industries such as personal computers, home electronics and automotives etc.A semiconductor is a material that is made of silicon or germanium or gallium arsenide, whose electrical conductivity falls between conductor and insulator . Conductor like \*\*copper\*\* and Insulator  like \*\*glass\*\*. It controls conductivity by allowing and restricting current flow by using external factors such as voltage, temperature. A semiconductor has both properties - insulator (substances which cannot pass electricity ) and conductor (substances which can pass electricity).This property makes it an ideal of technology because it is used in making computer chips, artificial intelligence chips and the internet of things (IoT). Semiconductors are very essential for us. #### The Discovery of Silicon
In the 19th century, The Swedish chemist Jones Jakob Berzelius who finally defined silicon in its pure form in 1824. and he was the person who gave its name i.e semimetal. That time it's become one of the defining semiconductor materials in electronics circuits. and also observed that its properties are as semiconductor and semimetal. That time it was not so important. And also they did not completely understand their importance. Early electrical engineers mostly used germanium as material instead of silicon.In 1874, Karl Ferdinand Braun was the person who discovered the rectifier effect. After that they understood the importance of semiconductors. And it became a very important and backbone of modern electronics devices. They used this to convert alternating current in Direct current. At the middle of the 20th century, semiconductor technology did not completely develop.
####  Transistor
In the beginning of 20th century, the three American scientists John Bardeen, Walter House Brattain and William Shockey developed a semiconductor that is Germanium's best device that leads the flow of electric current, they called it transistor. This was the first transistor which was invented in 1947. For this discovery, scientists were awarded the Nobel prize . Initially it was used in limited devices. Later it became the backbone of all modern electronics. Transistors were small in size and they consumed less current. It was widely used in electronic devices such as transistor radio. They have one disadvantage: they do not drive without power. After development of the field effect transistor, they become more powerful.

After some years, finally they made microprocessors and microcontrollers. In 1971 the first microprocessor finally came into the market, the Intel 4004. ##### Moore's Law
In 1965 Jordan Moore stated that in every two years on a microchip the number of transistors doubles and its cost becomes half at the same time. Moore’s law is not a real law or scientific law which has not proven science theories such as E = MC square. In 1965 when Moore worked on fairchild semiconductor. he observed that on a microchip the number of transistors doubled in every 2 years or about 18 months. Later he co-founded Intel corporation where his observation became Central to technology development. First he predicted that it would double in one year.

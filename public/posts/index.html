Introduction of Semiconductors

A  semiconductor is a material which acts as an insulator or conductor which is used in making electronic devices, such as blocks of computers and integrated circuits and also used in present digital technology. Semiconductors work as small electrical switches which repeatedly turn off and on the flow of electricity. It was widely used in many industries such as personal computers, home electronics and automotives etc.A semiconductor is a material that is made of silicon or germanium or gallium arsenide, whose electrical conductivity falls between conductor and insulator . Conductor like **copper** and Insulator  like **glass**. It controls conductivity by allowing and restricting current flow by using external factors such as voltage, temperature. A semiconductor has both properties - insulator (substances which cannot pass electricity ) and conductor (substances which can pass electricity).This property makes it an ideal of technology because it is used in making computer chips, artificial intelligence chips and the internet of things (IoT). Semiconductors are very essential for us. It is playing an important role in the rapid growth of technology.
